### 7. [Bloom Cycle x Brisbane Makerspace](https://forum.brisbanemaker.space/t/bloom-cycle-x-brisbane-makerspace/358)

Motion: BMS enters into an auspice arrangement with Bloom Cycle / Zoe. The conditions of this auspice arrangement are that Zoe is permitted to apply for grants on behalf of BMS, as long as any grant applications are sighted and approved by a member of the committee before being submitted. All grant payments should be paid into the BMS account, and any services Bloom Cycle / Zoe have been contracted to deliver on our behalf can be paid from those funds.

**For:** Avi, Drew, Jaimyn, Timothy, Stephanie, Claire, Sophie, Rosemary (everyone present)
**Against:** Nobody

The motion passes unanimously.

Motion: BMS enters into an agreement with Bloom Cycle / Zoe for a semi-permanent loan of her laser cutter with the following conditions:

> * It will become managed and cared for by BMS volunteers in line with our other digital fabrication tools.
> * BMS will be responsible for general maintenance, upgrades, managing inductions and similar, in consultation with Bloom Cycle / Zoe (though permission for said things cannot be unreasonably withheld). Bloom Cycle / Zoe have offered to cover the cost of installing the ducting for both machines (up to a reasonable amount as discussed).
> * It will operate under the standard laser cutter induction requirements, in addition to a minimum supervised hours requirement (to be agreed on between Zoe and BMS) before general members can use it.
> * Zoe will retain priority use of her machine while it sits on site, though this must not be unreasonably used.
> * In exchange for the laser cutter loan, Zoe is entitled to a free co-working desk and storage shelf in accordance with our co-working membership.

**For:** Avi, Drew, Jaimyn, Timothy, Stephanie, Claire, Sophie, Rosemary (everyone present)
**Against:** Nobody

The motion passes unanimously.
