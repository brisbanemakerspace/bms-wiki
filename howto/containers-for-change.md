# Donate your 10c Containers (Containers for Change)
Brisbane Makerspace (Meanjin Makerspace Inc.) is a registered charity with Containers for Change. This means when you return your 10c containers, instead of getting the cash back you can send it to BMS as a donation instead!

It's super easy to do so. When you return your containers, just scan our code or enter our member number (below). If you're returning them to a depot, just let the staff know you want to make a charity donation and show them the member number below.

That's it!

![bms-containers-for-change.png](/howto/bms-containers-for-change.png =500x)


## Print BMS bag return stickers
You can also download our 100x150mm sticker template to attach to your return bags. You can print these using an A6 sticker sheet or the shipping label printer at the space. This makes it easier for the staff to process, or if you want to use a bag drop.

[Download PDF]([bms_containers_for_change_return.pdf](/howto/bms_containers_for_change_return.pdf))

If you need to make changes, you can view the [original canva link](https://canva.link/bms-container-return-sticker) (requires BMS email account).
