# Why we have this policy
BMS is a community of like-minded makers who love socialising around our common love of making things. This includes members who produce creative works in many different forms.

Many members of our commuinty have had their work stolen or used without their consent to train generative AI. This policy aims to prevent poor uses of AI while supporting responsible use where it makes sense.

This policy tries to balance three things:
1. **A members' right to freely express themselves.** This means letting members choose what tools they use (including generative AI) for their own projects.
2. **Reducing the negative impacts of generative AI.** We want to limit the negative impacts of generative AI use at BMS.
3. **Keeping BMS authentically human and independent.** What we display around the space or publish as an organisation should focus on real people's work, not AI-generated content.

# What counts as "primarily AI-generated"
Content is considered primarily AI-generated when most of the final "work" came from a generative AI model. Put another way, if something is easily recognisable as "AI slop" or there's minimal human input - it's primarily AI-generated. 

Examples of primarily AI-generated work:
* illustrations, logos, artwork, video, voices or music made from a prompt with little human input except for crafting the prompt;
* written content (social media posts, policies, signs) where an AI wrote most of the words.
* something that is easily recognisable as "AI-slop" like entirely generated posters or large walls of text.

Examples of **not** primarily AI-generated work:
* A social media graphic made by assembling text, a background and some AI-generated icons.
* A tool induction that's filmed and edited by a person but uses an AI-generated script.
* A policy or email that was drafted with AI, but refined and reworded by a human.
* AI-generated spelling and grammar checks, basic photo edits like background removal, automatic captions, etc.

If you're still not sure, get consensus and ask a few members: *"Would a reasonable person say a human made this?"* If the answer is no, treat it as primarily AI-generated.

# BMS official content and social media
This applies to anything published or displayed **on behalf of BMS**, including our social media accounts, website, wiki, newsletters and emails, event listings (e.g. Meetup), posters, flyers, merchandise, grant applications, and signage around the space.

1. No primarily AI-generated text, images, video, audio or music.
2. Written content should be drafted by a person. AI-assisted editing is fine, but a human must write the substance and check everything before it's published.
3. When in doubt, leave it out. If you can't confirm where something came from or it's easily recognisable as AI, don't use it.
4. Disclose AI used in member works. If a project or artwork used AI, it must be clearly labelled as such (e.g. "made with AI-generated elements"). This disclosure isn't necessary for things like event graphics, etc.

The person publishing something is responsible for complying with this policy.

# Around the space
## Signs, posters, and projects on display
Things displayed around BMS can't be primarily AI-generated. This includes signage, posters, murals, wall art, decorations, and images.

If you spot something on display in breach of this policy, you're welcome to remove it under our [Do-ocracy](/bylaws/do-ocracy) principles. Never destroy or damage someone else's work, and make sure to let them know why it was removed and where they can find it.

## Members' own projects
Member's own projects can be primarily AI-generated content.

We don't like policing what individual members do (within reason) but please be respectful of other artists and creatives. Our other policies still apply, like the [Code of Conduct](/bylaws/coc) and [Safe Space](/bylaws/safespace) policies.

## Our online spaces
On Discord, the forum, and our other online spaces:
* Members can share their own projects (including primarily AI-generated ones).
* Don't post AI-generated content as spam, filler or in bulk. Remember, our [Moderation](/bylaws/moderation) policy still applies.
* We respectfully ask that you limit what AI-generated content you post because many members don't want to see it.
