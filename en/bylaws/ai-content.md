# Why we have this policy
Brisbane Makerspace is a community of people who make things with their hands, skills and imagination. Many of our members are artists, designers and crafters, and many of them have had their work used to train generative AI without consent or payment.

This policy tries to balance three things that matter to us:
1. **Members' freedom to make and express themselves**, including choosing their own tools for their own projects.
2. **The rights of artists and creators**, including credit, consent, and not having their work or style copied.
3. **Keeping BMS's own voice human-made.** The things BMS displays in the space or publishes as an organisation should celebrate real people's work, not AI-generated content.

In short: **what you make for yourself is up to you. What BMS shows and says as an organisation should be made by people.**

# What this policy covers
## What counts as "primarily AI-generated"
Content is **primarily AI-generated** when most of its creative expression came from a generative AI tool rather than a person. For example:
* images, illustrations, logos or artwork made from a text prompt (e.g. Midjourney, DALL·E, Stable Diffusion, Canva's "Magic" image tools);
* AI-generated video, voices or music;
* written content (posts, articles, signs) where an AI wrote most of the words.

## What doesn't count
Using AI as a **tool to assist human-made work** is not covered by this policy, as long as a person made the creative decisions and reviewed the result. For example:
* spelling and grammar checks, translation, or tidying up wording you wrote yourself;
* accessibility features like captions, transcripts, or alt text (please check them for accuracy);
* basic photo editing such as cropping, noise reduction, colour correction, or removing a background from a photo you took;
* technical tools like CAD, slicers, code assistants, or machine learning used *inside* a project (e.g. a robot that uses computer vision).

If you're not sure which side of the line something falls on, ask yourself: *"Would a reasonable person say a human made this?"* If the answer is no, treat it as primarily AI-generated.

> This is a policy about **AI-generated content**, not about AI as a technology. Workshops, projects, talks and discussions *about* AI, machine learning, or how these tools work are welcome at BMS.

# BMS official content and social media
This applies to anything published or displayed **on behalf of BMS**, including our social media accounts, website, wiki, newsletters and emails, event listings (e.g. Meetup), posters, flyers, merchandise, grant applications, and presentations given as BMS.

1. **No primarily AI-generated images, video, audio or music.** Use instead:
    * photos of our space, members, projects and events (with permission, see [Artists' and members' rights](#artists-and-members-rights));
    * artwork and designs made by members or other artists, credited and used with permission;
    * our Canva templates ([How to access our Canva account](/howto/canva)) and stock assets, but not AI image generators or "Magic" generation features;
    * simple text-based designs. A plain, honest post is better than a flashy AI one.
2. **Written content should be written by a person.** AI-assisted editing is fine (see [What doesn't count](#what-doesnt-count)), but a human must write the substance and check everything before it's published.
3. **When in doubt, leave it out.** If you can't confirm where an image came from (for example, stock images that might be AI-generated), don't use it.
4. **Sharing members' work.** If BMS shares a member's project or artwork that used AI, it must be clearly labelled as such (e.g. "made with AI-generated elements"), and the member must agree to it being shared.

The **Social Media Manager** is responsible for applying this policy to our social media accounts and has the final say on what's posted there. For other official content, the person publishing it is responsible, with the **Committee** as the final decision maker.

# In the physical space
## Shared and permanent displays
Things displayed in common areas on behalf of the space shouldn't be primarily AI-generated. This includes signage, posters, murals, wall art, decorations, labels, and information displays. This doesn't apply to text-only signs and labels made with ordinary software.

If you spot AI-generated content on a shared display, you're welcome to replace it with something human-made under our [Do-ocracy](/bylaws/do-ocracy). If it's someone else's work, check in with them or the Head Maker first.

## Members' own projects
Your own projects, workspace, belongings, laptop screen, and works in progress are **yours**. Nobody should police what tools you use for your personal projects, and this policy doesn't restrict that. (Our other policies still apply, like the [Code of Conduct](/bylaws/coc) and [Safe Space](/bylaws/safespace) policies.)

## Exhibitions, show-and-tells and events
* If you're showing work at a BMS exhibition, show-and-tell, market stall or similar event, **say if it is primarily AI-generated or uses significant AI-generated elements.**
* Event organisers may choose to run human-made-only events, such as an art show or craft market. If they do, they must say so clearly when they advertise the event or call for submissions.
* Workshops that teach AI tools are allowed, but the promotion for the workshop must follow the rules for [BMS official content](#bms-official-content-and-social-media).

# Member expression in our online spaces
On Discord, the forum, and other BMS online spaces:
* **You can share your projects, including ones that use AI.** Please label primarily AI-generated content clearly (e.g. "AI-generated" in your post). Don't pass it off as handmade.
* Channels or categories dedicated to art and crafts may be set as **human-made only** by the moderators. If so, it will be noted in the channel description.
* Don't post AI-generated content as spam, filler or reaction images in bulk. This is already covered by our [Moderation](/bylaws/moderation) policy.
* Disagreements about AI are fine, but criticise the content, not the person. Our [Code of Conduct](/bylaws/coc) applies.

# Artists' and members' rights
These apply everywhere: in the space, online, and in BMS official content.
1. **Credit people.** When you share someone else's work, name them. If you don't know who made it or don't have permission, don't use it.
2. **Get consent before sharing.** Ask before you photograph or post someone's project or artwork, or post photos of people.
3. **Don't feed other people's work into AI tools** (to train, remix, "restyle" or generate from) without their permission. This includes photos of members' projects and artwork taken in the space.
4. **Don't use AI to imitate a specific artist**, for example by prompting "in the style of [artist name]" or recreating a recognisable artist's or member's work for use at BMS or to share in our spaces.
5. **Members can ask for their work to be removed.** If your work, your likeness, or AI content based on your work appears on a BMS channel or in the space without your consent, contact the Social Media Manager (for social media) or the Committee and it will be removed as soon as practical.

# Handling concerns
* **Assume good intent.** Many people don't realise something was AI-generated, especially stock images and templates. Start with a friendly message, not an accusation.
* **Don't go on witch hunts.** It can be hard to tell whether something is AI-generated, and wrongly accusing someone, especially an artist, is hurtful. If you're unsure, ask the creator privately rather than calling it out publicly.
* **Who to contact:**
    * Social media and other official BMS content: the **Social Media Manager**.
    * Displays in the space: the **Head Maker**, or just fix it under our [Do-ocracy](/bylaws/do-ocracy).
    * Discord and the forum: flag the post for **moderators**.
    * Disputes or anything serious: the **Committee**. See [How to find the right person to contact](/howto/find-someone).
* **Consequences:** usually the content is just labelled, replaced or removed. Deliberately passing off AI-generated work as human-made, or repeatedly ignoring this policy or someone's request to remove their work, may be handled under our [Infractions](/bylaws/infractions) policy.

# Existing content
When this policy is adopted, existing primarily AI-generated content in shared displays and active templates (such as reusable Canva templates or recurring event banners) should be replaced over time. Older social media posts don't need to be deleted, but they shouldn't be reused or reshared.
